Hedgin's Solana program holds vault funds. Managers invest through approved actions on Jupiter, Meteora DLMM, and Phoenix Perpetuals. They cannot freely transfer vault funds to a personal wallet.

## Who controls what

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Depositor | Chooses a vault and requests deposits or withdrawals. |
| Manager | Chooses investments, sets vault fees and limits, and keeps funds available for withdrawals. Can refund unsettled requests. Private vault managers also approve requests. |
| Hedgin | Whitelists managers, sets protocol limits, and can pause activity or refund requests. |
| Valuation service | Calculates the vault's value and settles eligible requests every 4-hour window. It does not trade or close positions for the manager. |

The wallet that creates a vault remains its authority. That authority cannot be transferred to another wallet.

## Risks before you deposit

- **Investment losses.** Managers can lose some or all of the vault's funds through their trades.
- **Protocol risk.** Bugs or failures in Hedgin or its trading venues can cause losses.
- **Valuation risk.** Share prices depend on asset prices and position valuations. Missing data can delay settlement.
- **Withdrawal delays.** Withdrawals need available deposit tokens. They can wait while funds remain invested, activity is paused, or a private vault's manager has not approved the request.
- **Liquidation.** Leveraged Phoenix positions can lose all their collateral.

Trading restrictions do not guarantee returns or protect principal. Past performance does not predict future results.
