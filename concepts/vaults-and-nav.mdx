---

Each vault accepts one **deposit token**, such as USDC. When your deposit settles, you receive **shares** representing your stake in the vault. Withdrawals exchange those shares for the deposit token.

## Share prices

**Net asset value (NAV)** is the value of the vault's holdings. **NAV per share** is the value of one share in the deposit token. It changes as investments gain or lose value and fees accrue.

A new vault starts at 1 deposit token per share. At a settlement price of 1.25 USDC per share, a 1,000 USDC deposit receives approximately 800 shares. Withdrawing 800 shares at 1.30 USDC per share returns approximately 1,040 USDC. Amounts are rounded to token precision.

The website's live estimate can differ from the posted NAV. Requests use the posted settlement price, so their final amounts can differ from the estimate shown when you submit them.

### What the keeper counts

The **keeper**, Hedgin's valuation service, counts the vault's available deposit tokens, tracked token holdings, DLMM positions and trading fees, limit orders, and Phoenix account equity. Unclaimed Meteora farming rewards and untracked tokens sent directly to vault addresses are excluded.

If required prices or position data are unavailable, valuation can be delayed. Requests wait for a valid valuation.

## Settlement timing

An **epoch** is a fixed 4-hour window starting at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, or 20:00 UTC.

A request becomes eligible after a NAV is posted in a later epoch. For example, a request made at 09:30 UTC can settle after the valuation for the window starting at 12:00 UTC.

The keeper normally settles eligible requests automatically. Four hours is not a guaranteed completion time. Settlement can take longer when approval, valuation data, available funds, or vault settings prevent it.

A request can settle only during the epoch of its posted valuation. If that window passes, it waits for the next valuation.

## Limits and cancellations

- Each vault sets a deposit cap, minimum deposit, and minimum withdrawal. The deposit cap includes pending deposits.
- You can withdraw your whole share balance even when it is below the minimum withdrawal.
- You can have one open deposit request and one open withdrawal request per vault. Adding to a request in the same epoch increases it. An older request must close before you submit another of that kind.
- You can cancel before a later NAV locks the request. In a private vault, you can also cancel while awaiting manager approval. A deposit can be cancelled when NAV per share is zero.
- Adding to a request clears any approval, so a private vault's manager must approve the new amount.

Withdrawals need enough **idle balance**, meaning deposit tokens available outside investments. The keeper does not sell the manager's investments to fund them.

## Fees

Management and performance fees reduce the value of your shares. Review [Fees](/concepts/fees) and the vault's rates before depositing.

## What the status and pause switches block

| Status | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | Deposits, withdrawals, and trading are available, subject to limits and approvals. |
| Reduce-only | New deposits, pending deposit settlement, and new risk are blocked. Withdrawals and permitted exits remain available. |
| Paused | Deposits, withdrawals, and trading stop. Eligible request cancellation remains available. |

A locked deposit can remain unsettled until Normal status returns or the manager or Hedgin refunds it.

Separate deposit and withdrawal pause switches block both new requests and settlement for that request type. They do not block eligible cancellations.

Protocol-wide restrictions also apply. In particular, a protocol-wide reduce-only status blocks DLMM liquidity removal, fee claims, and limit order cancellation, even if the vault itself permits them.
