---
title: "Deposit and withdraw"
description: "Submit a request, follow settlement, or cancel before it locks."
---

Deposits and withdrawals settle after a valuation in a later 4-hour window. They are not immediate. Private vaults also require manager approval.

You need a Solana wallet, the vault's deposit token, and SOL for fees and account deposits. Review the vault's strategy, [risks](/concepts/protocol), [fees, and limits](/concepts/vaults-and-nav) before depositing.

## Deposit

1. Open a vault at [hedgin.xyz/vaults](https://hedgin.xyz/vaults) and connect your wallet.
2. Select **Deposit** and enter the amount.
3. Review the estimated shares, then select **Request deposit** and approve.
4. Follow the request card until settlement. Your shares normally arrive automatically. If **Claim shares** is available, you can claim them yourself.

Your tokens wait in a holding account until settlement and are not invested during that wait. The final share amount uses the settlement price, which can differ from the estimate.

For SOL vaults, the website handles conversion between SOL and wrapped SOL.

## Withdraw

1. On the vault page, select **Withdraw**.
2. Enter the number of shares, or select **Max** for your full balance.
3. Review the estimated payout, then select **Request withdrawal** and approve.
4. Wait for settlement. You receive the vault's deposit token, or native SOL for a SOL vault. If a claim button is available, you can claim it yourself.

The final payout uses the settlement price. You can withdraw your full balance even when it is below the vault's minimum withdrawal.

<Note>
Withdrawals depend on available funds. If the vault's deposit tokens are invested, the manager must free enough cash first. The keeper does not close positions to pay withdrawals.
</Note>

## Follow your requests

The vault page shows your request's progress. [Portfolio](https://hedgin.xyz/portfolio) lists your positions and requests across vaults.

| Request state | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting approval | Wait for the private vault's manager, or cancel. |
| Waiting for valuation | Wait for a valuation in a later 4-hour window. |
| Ready to claim or processing | The keeper normally settles it. Use the claim button if available. |
| Delayed or claim on hold | Read the reason on the request card. It may need a new valuation, available funds, approval, or a pause to end. |

Settlement is not guaranteed within four hours. If a request misses its settlement window, it waits for another valuation and uses that valuation's price.

You can have one open deposit request and one open withdrawal request per vault. Adding to a request in the same window increases its amount and clears any manager approval. A request from an older window must close first.

## Cancel a request

On the vault page, select **Cancel request** and approve. Your held tokens or shares return to your wallet.

Cancellation is available before a later valuation locks the request. In private vaults, it also remains available while awaiting manager approval. You can cancel a deposit if NAV per share is zero. These cancellations work during a pause.

## If a request cannot proceed

| Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Below the minimum deposit | Increase the amount. |
| Deposit cap reached | Reduce the amount or wait for capacity. Pending deposits count toward the cap. |
| Deposits or withdrawals paused | Wait for them to reopen. |
| Vault or protocol reduce-only | New deposits and pending deposit settlement are blocked. A locked deposit waits until Normal status returns or the manager or Hedgin refunds it. |
| Older request still open | Settle or cancel it when allowed. |
| Manager or Hedgin rejects the request | Your held tokens or shares return to your wallet. |

If an action is pending, unknown, or failed, follow [Pending or failed actions](/faq#pending-or-failed-actions) before repeating it.
