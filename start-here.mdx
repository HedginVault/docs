## Deposit into a vault

You need a Solana wallet, the vault's accepted token, and SOL for fees.

1. Browse [vaults](https://hedgin.xyz/vaults) and choose a strategy you understand.
2. Review its holdings, fees, limits, and approval requirements.
3. Connect your wallet and [request a deposit](/depositors/deposits-and-withdrawals#deposit).

Your deposit waits for settlement before shares arrive. See [Shares and pricing](/concepts/vaults-and-nav) for timing.

## Become a manager

Use a dedicated wallet. The wallet that creates a vault remains its authority and cannot transfer that authority to another wallet.

1. Ask Hedgin to whitelist your wallet's public address.
2. Fund the wallet with SOL for account deposits and fees.
3. Open the [manager console](https://hedgin.xyz/manage) and [create a vault](/managers/overview#create-a-vault).

Once the vault is ready, you can manage [swaps](/managers/jupiter-swaps), [liquidity](/managers/meteora-dlmm), and [perpetuals](/managers/phoenix-perps).

## Automate a vault

Use the [SDK getting-started guide](/sdk/getting-started) for TypeScript or the [API getting-started guide](/api-reference/getting-started) for HTTP integrations.

These integrations support manager actions. Use the website to submit depositor requests or settle them.
