Phoenix perpetual futures let a USDC vault take long or short positions with leverage. Use **Perps** in the [manager console](https://hedgin.xyz/manage).

<Warning>
Leverage magnifies losses. All positions share collateral in the vault's Phoenix account. Liquidation can consume all collateral in that account.
</Warning>

## Set up trading

1. Select **Create account** and approve.
2. Select **Activate trading** and approve.
3. Use **Add collateral** to move the vault's idle USDC into Phoenix.

Each vault has one Phoenix account. Its collateral and open profit or loss count toward vault valuation.

## Place and manage orders

1. Choose a market and **Long** or **Short**.
2. Choose **Market** for an immediate trade or **Limit** to specify a price.
3. Enter the size and leverage.
4. Review the estimated fill, liquidation price, account leverage, and fees before submitting.

Use **Reduce only** to shrink a position without opening or reversing one. Use **Post only** when a limit order must rest on the order book rather than execute immediately.

Review open positions under **Positions** and cancel resting orders under **Open orders**. Monitor account margin as well as individual positions.

## Withdraw collateral

Use **Remove collateral** to return available funds to the vault. Phoenix can queue a withdrawal. Once returned funds are available, select **Unwrap** to make them available as idle USDC.

Funds still in Phoenix or awaiting return cannot pay depositor withdrawals.

## Trading restrictions

Normal status permits trading. Reduce-only permits collateral withdrawals, order cancellation, unwrapping, and reduce-only market orders. It blocks new risk and collateral deposits. Paused status blocks Phoenix actions.

## Wind down the account

1. Close all positions.
2. Cancel all open orders.
3. Remove all collateral.
4. Wait for queued withdrawals and unwrap returned funds.
5. In **Portfolio**, select **Close strategy** once the account is empty.

Phoenix trader accounts cannot be closed, so their account deposit is not refunded.

To automate Phoenix actions, use the [SDK examples](/sdk/examples) or [Manager API](/api-reference/introduction).
