Hedgin can message you when a vault you manage needs action. Alerts go to the vault's manager wallet only. Delegates do not receive them.

## Connect a channel

Open the bell in the manager console and choose **Delivery Settings**. Sign in with your manager wallet first.

- **Telegram:** select **Connect**. Telegram opens the Hedgin bot. Select **Start**. The link works once and expires after 10 minutes. To stop alerts from the chat, send `/alerts_off`.
- **Email:** enter your address and select **Connect**. Enter the 6-digit code from the email and select **Verify**. The code expires after 10 minutes. You can request a new code after the countdown ends. Five wrong codes cancel it.

Emails come from `notification@hedgin.xyz`. Each email has an unsubscribe link.

Select **Send test alert** to check that your channels work.

## Alerts and what to do

Each alert has its own switch. A switch applies to Telegram and email. The bell always shows every alert.

| Alert | When | What to do | Starts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Withdrawals short | Queued withdrawals, priced at the last NAV, exceed the vault's idle balance | Close or reduce a strategy position so idle funds cover the payout | On |
| Valuation stalled | The keeper has failed to value the vault for 30 minutes | Check the named error, then contact Hedgin if an override is needed | On |
| Guardian paused | A guardian paused the vault | Contact Hedgin | On |
| Withdrawals waiting | Queued withdrawals that idle funds already cover | Nothing. The keeper pays them after the next NAV | Off |

Hedgin checks every 5 minutes and sends each alert once. If a problem is still open in the next 4-hour epoch, you get one reminder for that epoch.
