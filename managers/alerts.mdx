Hedgin can message you about deposit and withdrawal activity on the vaults you manage. Alerts go to the vault's manager wallet only. Delegates do not receive them.

## Connect a channel

Open the bell in the manager console and choose **Delivery Settings**. Sign in with your manager wallet first.

- **Telegram:** select **Connect**. Telegram opens the Hedgin bot. Select **Start**. The link works once and expires after 10 minutes. To stop alerts from the chat, send `/alerts_off`.
- **Email:** enter your address and select **Connect**. Enter the 6-digit code from the email and select **Verify**. The code expires after 10 minutes. You can request a new code after the countdown ends. Five wrong codes cancel it.

Emails come from `notification@hedgin.xyz`. Each email has an unsubscribe link.

Select **Send test alert** to check that your channels work.

## Alerts and what to do

There are two switches, **Deposits** and **Withdrawals**. Both start on. A switch applies to Telegram and email.

| Switch | You get an alert when | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deposits | A depositor requests a deposit | Nothing. It settles at the next valuation. You can reject it from the console before then |
| Deposits | A deposit request is cancelled, settled or rejected | Nothing |
| Withdrawals | A depositor requests a withdrawal | Keep enough of the deposit token idle for the next valuation to pay it |
| Withdrawals | A withdrawal request is cancelled, paid or rejected | Nothing |
| Withdrawals | Queued withdrawals, priced at the last NAV, exceed the vault's idle balance | Swap or close positions so idle funds cover the payout |

Each alert shows the amount, the depositor's address for a single request, and a button to open the vault console. Requests settled in the same transaction arrive as one alert.

Hedgin checks every 5 minutes and sends each alert once. Activity older than 2 hours is not sent. If idle funds are still short in the next 4-hour epoch, you get one reminder for that epoch.

Valuation problems and guardian pauses are not sent by Telegram or email. They show in the bell.
