Use the [manager console](https://hedgin.xyz/manage) to run your vault. Hedgin must whitelist your wallet before you can create one.

You choose the investments and remain responsible for losses and withdrawal liquidity. Read [Custody and risks](/concepts/protocol) and [Vaults, shares, and NAV](/concepts/vaults-and-nav) before accepting deposits.

## Prepare the manager wallet

Use a dedicated wallet and fund it with SOL for account deposits and network fees. Send its public address to Hedgin to request whitelisting, then connect it to the console.

<Warning>
The wallet that creates a vault remains its authority. You cannot transfer that authority to another wallet. Losing or exposing the key puts control of the vault at risk.
</Warning>

## Create a vault

On **Your vaults**, select **New vault**, complete the fields, then select **Create vault** and approve.

| Field | What to choose |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Up to 32 bytes. |
| Deposit token | USDC, USDT, or SOL. This cannot change later. Phoenix requires USDC. |
| Performance fee | Up to 50% of eligible profit. |
| Management fee | Up to 5% per year. |
| Limits | Deposit cap, minimum deposit, and minimum withdrawal shares. |
| Private vault | Requires your approval for each deposit and withdrawal request. |

A new vault starts at 1 deposit token per share.

## Describe the strategy

In **Settings**, edit the vault's **Profile** so depositors understand your strategy. Save the profile with your wallet.

Use **Manager profile** on **Your vaults** to set your manager name and X handle.

## Invest the vault

| Venue | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [Jupiter](/managers/jupiter-swaps) | Swap vault tokens. |
| [Meteora DLMM](/managers/meteora-dlmm) | Provide liquidity, collect trading fees, and place limit orders. |
| [Phoenix Perpetuals](/managers/phoenix-perps) | Trade leveraged long and short positions from a USDC vault. |

Use **Portfolio** to review holdings and exit positions. Use **History** to review closed strategies and their results.

## Manage requests and withdrawal liquidity

Check **Requests** regularly. Keep enough uninvested deposit tokens to cover withdrawals. Sell tokens or remove liquidity when more cash is needed. The keeper does not close positions for you.

For private vaults, approve or reject requests in **Requests**. A manager or Hedgin can refund an unsettled request on any vault, returning the depositor's held tokens or shares. Adding to an approved request clears its approval, so review the updated amount.

The keeper normally settles eligible requests after valuation. If a ready request remains, use its settlement action in **Requests**. Requests that miss the current valuation window wait for the next one.

## Change settings and fees

Edit fees, limits, status, and deposit or withdrawal pauses under **Settings**, then save and approve.

- If either fee increases, both requested rates wait at least **2 days** and activate at a subsequent valuation. Updates that only lower fees apply immediately.
- **Reduce only** blocks new deposits, pending deposit settlement, and new risk while allowing withdrawals and permitted exits.
- **Paused** stops deposits, withdrawals, and trading.
- The deposit and withdrawal pause switches block new requests and settlement for the selected type.

Protocol-wide restrictions can also block actions. See [Status and pause rules](/concepts/vaults-and-nav#what-the-status-and-pause-switches-block).

## Claim your fees

Under **Your fees** in **Portfolio**, select **Claim fees** and approve. Fees arrive as vault shares. To receive deposit tokens, withdraw those shares through the normal request process.

## Close strategies and the vault

Exit holdings before closing their strategies.

| Holding | Exit |
| --- | --- |
| Swapped token | Use **Sell all & close**. Close DLMM positions or orders using that token first. |
| DLMM position | Use **Zap out**, or remove liquidity, claim fees, and close the position. |
| DLMM limit order | Cancel the order to return remaining tokens, proceeds, and fees. |
| Phoenix | Close positions, cancel orders, withdraw collateral, and unwrap returned funds before closing the strategy. |

To close the vault, every strategy and request must be closed. Share supply, unclaimed fee shares, total assets, and idle balance must all be zero. Claim and withdraw your own fee shares too.

Open **Settings**, expand the vault closure panel, and complete its checks before confirming. Closing a vault is permanent.

## Automate management

Create a vault-scoped API key under **Connect AI**. Choose only the actions the bot needs. Self-created keys expire after 90 days.

Use the [TypeScript SDK](/sdk/overview), [Manager API](/api-reference/introduction), or [Telegram bot](/managers/telegram-bot). The API does not support depositor requests, request settlement, or profile editing. Keep API and signing keys out of prompts, logs, and URLs.
