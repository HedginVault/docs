Hedgin runs investment vaults on Solana. Depositors put one token, such as USDC, into a vault and receive shares. A whitelisted manager invests the vault through Jupiter swaps, Meteora DLMM liquidity, and Phoenix perpetuals. Every 4 hours, Hedgin values each vault, and waiting deposits and withdrawals settle at that price.

A Solana program holds the vault's tokens. The manager can trade them only through those three venues and cannot send them anywhere else. Depositors get tokens back only to their own wallet.

<Note>
Hedgin runs on Solana mainnet. Every transaction moves real funds. There is no public test deployment.
</Note>
