## What is Hedgin?

Hedgin connects depositors with vault managers. You deposit a token, such as USDC, into a vault. The manager invests it, and you hold shares that reflect the vault's value.

Managers trade through Jupiter, Meteora DLMM, and Phoenix Perpetuals. Vault funds are held by Hedgin's Solana program, with defined permissions for trading and withdrawals.

## Before you deposit

Your shares gain or lose value with the vault's investments. Deposits and withdrawals settle after a valuation in a later 4-hour window. Withdrawals also depend on available funds and any manager approval requirements.

Read about [shares and pricing](/concepts/vaults-and-nav), review the [fees](/concepts/fees), and understand the [risks](/concepts/protocol).
